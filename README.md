# Rock Paper Scissors

A small rock-paper-scissors game you play in the browser against the computer.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in any browser. You don't need to install or build anything.

- Click **Rock**, **Paper** or **Scissors**, or press <kbd>R</kbd>, <kbd>P</kbd> or <kbd>S</kbd>.
- The computer picks a move at random and the winner is shown.
- The scoreboard keeps track of wins, losses and draws. The colored dots show your last 20 rounds.
- Click **Reset score** to start over.

## Rules

- Rock crushes scissors
- Paper covers rock
- Scissors cuts paper
- The same move on both sides is a draw
